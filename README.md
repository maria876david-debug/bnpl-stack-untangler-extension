# BNPL Stack Untangler — Payment Quick Total

Total what you still owe across Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm plans.

A pocket companion for BNPL Stack Untangler, the free tool that puts all your Klarna, Afterpay and Affirm payments on one calendar. Total what you still owe across plans in seconds. No bank login, no account, nothing leaves this popup.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/bnpl-stack-untangler-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://bnplstackuntangler.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
