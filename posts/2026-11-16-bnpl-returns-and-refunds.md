# BNPL Returns and Refunds: What Happens to Your Plan When You Send Something Back

Returning a buy-now-pay-later purchase does not always cancel the plan by itself. In many cases the refund goes back to the lender first, and your remaining instalments only stop once the refund is processed - which can take days. If you already paid an instalment, that money usually comes back too, but the timing depends on the store and the provider.

The safe steps: keep the return receipt, check your plan status after the refund lands, and contact the provider if an instalment is still charged after the return is complete. A returned item should never keep costing you money. BNPL Stack Untangler explains how returns, refunds and stacked plans work, so a simple return does not turn into a surprise charge.
