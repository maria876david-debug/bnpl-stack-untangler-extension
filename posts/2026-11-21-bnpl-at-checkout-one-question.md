# BNPL at Checkout: The One Question to Ask Before You Tap Pay Later

The pay-later button at checkout takes one tap, and that is exactly the problem. Before you tap it, ask one question: when is every single payment due, and does it clash with anything else I already owe that week? A plan that costs nothing extra is only free if every payment lands on time - one missed date adds a late fee, and two missed dates can close the plan or damage your record with the provider. The amount is rarely the issue; most plans are small. The issue is the calendar: four plans from four shops, each with its own app and its own due date, and none of them talking to each other. Write the dates down before you buy, in one list, and check the week the payments fall in. If the money is not there that week, the button is not free - it is a loan with a deadline.
