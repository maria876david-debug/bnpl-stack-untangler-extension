# Does BNPL Affect Your Credit Score in 2027? What Each App Reports, App by App

There is no single answer — it depends on which app the plan lives in. Affirm, the provider that charges no late fees at all, reports the most: your entire payment history, shared with Experian and TransUnion, with a payment 30 or more days past due reported as late. By contrast, Klarna and Afterpay keep their Pay in 4 plans off your credit report under their current terms. Nothing in that map has been announced to change for 2027.

Zip says it may report information about your account, and late or missed payments may be reflected in your credit report. Sezzle reports standard Pay in 4 plans only if you opt in to Sezzle Up, although its monthly plans may be reported. PayPal says Pay in 4 has no impact on your credit, and applying uses a soft credit check only. Klarna is split by product: Pay in 4 payments are not shared, while its monthly financing is shared with TransUnion and Experian.

On-time payments build credit only where the provider reports them: Affirm payments are on the record, and Sezzle Up opt-in payments can be too, while Klarna Pay in 4, Afterpay and PayPal Pay in 4 leave no trace. The habit itself can still leave a long record: in one case on this site, a shopper who paid in full every month — about four purchases a week over three years, up to $1,000 a month — found close to 600 settled BNPL accounts on her credit report, taking almost 30 seconds of scrolling to reach the end.
