# One List for Every BNPL Plan: The Five-Minute Habit That Stops Missed Payments

Most missed BNPL payments are not a money problem - they are a memory problem. Each plan lives in its own app, with its own due date, and nothing shows you the whole picture at once. The fix takes five minutes: one list, in one place, with every plan, its remaining balance and its due date, in date order. A notes app is enough. Check it every Friday and you will see a clash coming two weeks early - the week three plans land on the same payday - while there is still time to move money or contact the provider. People do not miss payments because the amount is large; the amounts are usually small. They miss because five small dates are harder to hold in your head than one big one. Write them down once, and the problem mostly disappears.
