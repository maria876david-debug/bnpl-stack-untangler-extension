# BNPL and Your Credit Score: What Actually Gets Reported

The question almost every BNPL user asks is whether these plans touch their credit score. The honest answer is: it depends on the provider and on what happens. Many pay-in-four plans do not report your on-time payments to the credit bureaus, so paying on time does not build your score the way a credit card does. But a missed payment can be a different story — some providers report late payments, and an unpaid balance sent to collections will show up. The rule that keeps you safe is simple: treat every BNPL plan like a real bill, keep the total number of open plans small, and never stack a new plan on top of a payment you are already unsure about.
