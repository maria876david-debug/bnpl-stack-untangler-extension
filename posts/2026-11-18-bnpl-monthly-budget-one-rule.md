# BNPL and Your Monthly Budget: One Simple Rule for Every Plan

Buy now, pay later only works if every plan fits inside your monthly budget - and the simplest way to check that is one rule: the total of all your BNPL instalments due in a month should never be more than you can cover after rent, food and bills are paid. Add up every plan before you open a new one. If the sum already feels tight, the new plan is not affordable, however small its first payment looks. The trap is not one big plan; it is five small ones whose due dates land in the same week. Write every due date down in one place, set a reminder two days before each one, and treat a missed payment like a missed rent payment - because the fees and the credit consequences are just as real.
