# BNPL Rules in 2027: The Fees, Credit Reporting and Plan Rules Shoppers Will Actually Feel

First, the honest news: none of the six providers tracked here — Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm, PayPal, Zip and Sezzle — has published a new 2027 fee schedule or credit-reporting policy in the pages checked in November 2026. The rules carrying into the new year are the rules already in force, so treat this as a baseline, not a round of guesses.

On fees, Klarna charges up to $7 per late Pay in 4 payment, once it is still unpaid 10 days after the due date, with total late fees on an order capped at 25% of the purchase amount. Afterpay charges up to $8 per late instalment after a grace period of usually 10 days, also capped at 25%. Sezzle charges up to $16.95, capped at 25% of the order, and Zip charges $7. Affirm and PayPal Pay in 4 charge no late fee.

Reporting does not line up with the fees. Affirm shares your entire payment history with Experian and TransUnion, and a payment 30 or more days past due is reported as late. Zip may report account information, and late or missed payments may show. Klarna does not share Pay in 4 payments, though its monthly financing is shared. Afterpay does not currently report in the United States, Sezzle reports standard Pay in 4 plans only if you opt in to Sezzle Up, and PayPal says Pay in 4 has no impact on your credit.
