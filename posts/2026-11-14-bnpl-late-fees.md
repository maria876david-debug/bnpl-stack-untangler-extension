# BNPL Late Fees: What Happens When You Miss a Payment?

Buy Now, Pay Later sounds free — until a payment is missed. Then late fees apply, and they differ by provider. Our late-fees guide compares the major US providers side by side, using each provider's own published terms.

One example: Afterpay in the US charges an $8 late fee, gives a 10-day grace period, and caps total late fees at 25% of the order value. Other providers set their own limits, so the cost of the same missed payment is not the same everywhere.

Before you split a purchase, check what a missed payment would actually cost. Read the guide at https://bnplstackuntangler.com/guides/bnpl-late-fees
