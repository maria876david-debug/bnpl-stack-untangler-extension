# How to Clear a BNPL Stack in 2027: The List, the Payoff Order and Your Debt-Free Date

Paying on time does not clear a stack by itself. A single Pay in 4 plan runs about six weeks and then closes, but another plan can open underneath it, so the set of open plans across all your apps can stay the same size for months. No app shows you that whole set — Klarna shows Klarna, Afterpay shows Afterpay — so the total, the calendar and the end date stay invisible until you write them down.

Start with one list, and allow about 20 minutes for a typical stack. For every open plan, note the provider, what you bought, how much each payment is, how many payments are left and the next due date. Add up the total still owed, count the payments remaining, and work out how much leaves your account before your next payday. Line the payments up against your paydays and flag the periods where several land together, then set aside the amount for each tight week from the paycheck before it.

Your debt-free date is the day the last payment of the last plan leaves your account, if every payment lands on schedule. Keep every plan on schedule: a missed payment is how fees begin — up to $7 at Klarna, up to $8 at Afterpay and up to $16.95 at Sezzle — and at Affirm and Zip a late payment can reach your credit report. Send extra money to one plan at a time, either the smallest balance first or the plan that clashes worst with your payday.
